Flagship Project Production Point of Sale

Hardware Store POS & Inventory Management System

Project Overview

The Hardware Store POS & Inventory Management System is a full-stack, enterprise-grade retail platform developed specifically for daily hardware and construction supply operations. Unlike generic retail software, hardware retail demands multi-unit conversions (boxes, pieces, meters, kilograms), rapid keyboard-driven checkout, real-time stock balance tracking, supplier replenishment pipelines, and dedicated customer credit ledger tracking (pautang).

Architected with a modern client-server stack using React, TypeScript, Node.js, and MySQL, the system guarantees high-speed transaction processing, instant barcode scanner integration, and real-time inventory ledger synchronization across cash registers and warehouse computers.

Key Features & Engineering Highlights
  • Keyboard-First Cashier Terminal: Ergonomic checkout flow equipped with hotkeys (F1–F12, Enter, Esc), allowing cashiers to ring up orders, modify quantities, and process payments without touching the mouse.
  • Barcode Scanning & Fuzzy Search: Instant SKU detection via physical handheld barcode scanners, supplemented with sub-millisecond keyword and category search.
  • Live Stock Ledger & Auditing: Complete chronological audit log of every stock addition, counter deduction, damage write-off, and supplier return to prevent discrepancy.
  • Customer Credit & Utang Ledger: Tracks customer credit limits, billing statements, partial repayments, and outstanding balance aging reports.
  • Real-Time Multi-Terminal Sync: WebSocket events broadcast stock changes and cash drawer events across concurrent stations instantly.
  • Thermal Receipt & Invoice Printing: Native formatting for ESC/POS thermal printers with automated sales summary receipts and official customer invoices.
Technology Stack

Isra Hardware Trading Website

Project Overview

Isra Hardware Trading Website is a full-stack business website developed to establish a professional online presence for Isra Hardware Trading. It provides customers with an accessible way to browse hardware and construction products, explore product categories, find store information, locate the physical shop, and communicate directly with the business.

The platform features a modern storefront interface, an integrated interactive customer service chat assistant designed for real-time quotation assistance, and an administrative Customer Helpdesk & Quotation Desk allowing store staff to triage inquiries, verify warehouse stock, and send prompt replies.

Core Objectives & Features
  • Responsive Digital Storefront: Extends the reach of the local hardware store to online shoppers, contractors, and builders across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
  • Interactive Customer Chat Box: Conversational quotation and product inquiry assistant allowing customers to check stock, ask store staff questions, and request official quotations.
  • Admin Customer Helpdesk & Quotation Desk: Dedicated back-office dashboard to triage customer chats, review AI assistant escalations, verify warehouse inventory stock in real time, and dispatch tailored quotations.
  • Structured Product Catalog: Filterable category hierarchy for power tools, plumbing, electrical, paints, masonry, and raw construction materials.
  • Store Locator & Information: Embedded Google Maps locator, physical store address, operating hours, and direct telephone/WhatsApp links.
  • Cloud-Based Architecture & Edge Delivery: High-performance serverless deployment on Cloudflare Workers edge network with Supabase PostgreSQL and Storage for blazing-fast asset delivery.
  • Admin & Content Management: Administrative portal to update catalog products, adjust inventory, manage categories, and handle customer requests.

CENRO Waste Tracker System

Project Overview

CENRO Waste Tracker System is a municipal solid waste management and route monitoring platform engineered for the Community Environment & Natural Resources Office (CENRO). The platform unifies administrative logistics, real-time driver navigation, and citizen participation into a single end-to-end ecosystem.

The system comprises a desktop web administrative terminal, cross-platform mobile dispatch tools for sanitation truck drivers, and public reporting portals to optimize waste collection schedules, reduce fuel overhead, and eliminate missed pickups.

Core Objectives & Features
  • Admin Terminal & Route Dispatch: Centralized dashboard for city environmental officers to monitor active routes, track completed vs. pending collections, and visualize live collection status.
  • Interactive Route GIS Mapping: Real-time map displaying municipal zones, collection progress, road blockages, and truck positions.
  • Mobile Collection Driver App (Flutter): Native cross-platform Android app for waste truck drivers featuring live route navigation, interactive checkpoint tracking, on-duty status dispatching, and equipment issue reporting.
  • Citizen Live Map & Reporting Portal: Public mobile interface allowing residents to track real-time waste collection progress, view approaching garbage truck locations on the map, and report uncollected bins or illegal dumping hotspots.
  • Real-Time Data Sync: Powered by PostgreSQL and Firebase for sub-second synchronization of vehicle coordinates, status changes, and notifications.
  • Analytics & Environmental Auditing: Generates automated compliance reports, collection efficiency indices, and driver activity logs.
