Flagship Project Completed / Production

Hardware Store POS System

Purpose: Built to solve inventory mismanagement, manual checkout delays, and unrecorded bulk commodity trades in a local hardware and construction supply business.

What I Developed:

Architected and developed a full-stack, enterprise-grade Point of Sale and retail operations system as a monorepo. The application pairs a responsive React and TypeScript frontend with a high-performance Express/Node.js backend and a normalized MySQL relational database, leveraging shared TypeScript types between layers for end-to-end type safety.

Key Implemented Features:
  • Cashier & POS Terminal: Rapid checkout workflow with automated total calculations, discounts, and item lookups.
  • Barcode Scanner Integration: Hardware scanner compatibility for zero-friction SKU barcode lookup.
  • Real-Time Inventory Management: Automatic stock level deductions, low-stock threshold alerts, and batch adjustments.
  • Customer Accounts & Credit: Customer balance tracking, sales on credit, and payment ledger history.
  • Specialized Copra & Charcoal Tracking: Custom module built specifically for the client's dual trade in agricultural commodities.
  • WebSockets Real-Time Sync: Instant notification of register transactions across terminals without page refresh.
  • Authentication & Role Security: JWT authentication with distinct Cashier vs. Manager permissions.
  • Financial Auditing & Exports: Daily sales reporting with exportable reports in Excel (.xlsx) and PDF formats.
